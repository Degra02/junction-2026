Is this manipulation, or one voice in a network?

Answering that today is hours of manual OSINT: read for techniques, find other coverage, look for fact-checks, check the domain, search for copies, compare tracking codes.

Why not just ask an AI?

Its opinion of the text alone is not evidence: biased by language, prompt-injectable, and nobody can check it.

A confidence and its evidence. Never a verdict.

  • No “propaganda / not propaganda” label: that would pose as ground truth.
  • “Not enough evidence”, with the reason, instead of a guess.
  • Every reason links to its source; every quote is checked word for word, or dropped.
  • An LLM never sets the score.
