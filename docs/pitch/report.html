1. Summary

  • What it does. Paste a text, a link or an image. The pipeline extracts the content, works out what it claims, finds how other outlets, fact-checkers and copies covered the same event, and answers two separate questions: how strongly the content shows signs of propaganda by its author (single-actor) and of coordinated propaganda (the same message pushed by outlets acting together).
  • What it never does. It never returns a “propaganda / not propaganda” label (decision D4). Each dimension gets a confidence with its evidence, or “Not enough evidence” with the reason. No LLM sets a score: the score is a logistic regression over measured signal families, and every quotation is checked word for word by the API.
  • How well it works on 284 items labelled by third parties (fact-checkers, EUvsDisinfo, VIGINUM) in 12 languages:
    • Single-actor: precision 0.98 [0.91–1.00] and false-positive rate 0.03 [0.00–0.13] on the items it scores; it scores 43% of items and abstains on the rest, so it catches 46% of all positives. Cross-validated AUC 0.98, 0.84 without fact-check matches.
    • Coordinated: perfect on the set, but only because the VIGINUM list both labels the positives and is a signal. Without it the cross-validated AUC is 0.38: today the coordinated score is the list, not copy detection. We publish that next to the headline.
  • Beyond the plan. A source graph of 908 outlets tied by shared analytics IDs, copied and translated text (live at /sources), and phase 1 of persuasion analysis for memes (a multimodal rater whose evidence is shown but not yet scored).
  • Runs live on one CPU-only Hostinger VPS; a full analysis takes about a minute (median 64 s).

2. Problem and product

A reader sees an article or a post and cannot tell whether it is manipulation by its author, or one node of a network pushing the same story. Answering that today is manual OSINT: read for techniques, find other coverage, check fact-checkers, look up the domain, search for copies, compare tracking IDs. A model's opinion of the text alone is not evidence: it is biased by language, can be prompt-injected, and the reader cannot check it.

The product does the OSINT and shows its work. Inspiration is Ground News's story-level comparison (an item judged against its peers on the same event), without third-party political-bias ratings (D9): we use measurable outlet types instead (news agency, fact-checker, satire, national or local newsroom, state-affiliated).

3. Architecture and pipeline

A Rust API (axum, SQLite) owns jobs, the queue, progress over server-sent events, caching, re-runs and replays, and validates every stage's output against the contract. It calls one Python worker (FastAPI) per stage with the full analysis record; each stage returns a patch of its own fields. The contract is written as Rust types and generated into Python (pydantic) and TypeScript; CI fails on drift.

StageOwnerDoes
extractck-lithiumText, URL or image → paragraphs, outlet, date, author, tracking IDs; language (lingua) with support tier; images read by a vision model, including what the image implies
understandLyreplusOne LLM call: summary, entities, up to 5 verbatim one-fact claims, implied claims (memes, sarcasm), search queries in the item's language and English
retrieveck-lithiumThe only web I/O after extract: curated outlet feeds, GDELT and Oxylabs exact-phrase and news search, Google Fact Check Tools; full article bodies; a profile per outlet (RDAP registration, first certificate, outlet type, country, VIGINUM membership)
storyck-lithiumMultilingual e5 embeddings: which retrieved items cover the same event (threshold 0.90 same language, 0.845 across languages, date window)
signals_single / coordinatedLyreplusEvidence items per family, each with source references; no web access
scoreLyreplusFitted combiner, abstention rules, per-family breakdown in log-odds
explainLyreplusOne LLM call: summary and three reasons from the evidence only, optionally in the reader's language (D14); never changes the score
graphLyreplusIngests the analysis into the live source graph (outlets, articles, IDs, ties)

Contract validation by the API. A stage fails on duplicate IDs, evidence outside its dimension or an invalid score. Claims that are not verbatim at their span, evidence without a source reference or with a non-verbatim quote, and reasons quoting text that is not in the document, the context or an evidence detail are dropped and listed in the step's detail.

The combiner

confidence = σ( b + Σf wf · vf )

Each family value vf ∈ [−1, 1] is the strength of its strongest signal only, so correlated evidence is not counted twice; negative values point away from propaganda. The weights are a non-negative logistic regression fitted on the labelled set (L2 toward hand-set priors, 5-fold cross-validation). The contributions wf·vf are the numbers shown to the reader in “Show how we calculated this”.

Abstention (“Not enough evidence”)

  • Both dimensions: no text, or an unsupported language. A supported but not evaluated language caps confidence at 0.85 (D2).
  • Single-actor: a satire outlet; fewer than 3 related reports and no fact-check match.
  • Coordinated: no copies, no listed domain and fewer than 3 items found.
  • Either: one family ≥ 0.7 while another ≤ −0.7 (“evidence points both ways”).

Read the headline with its caveats.

  • Coordinated is the VIGINUM list today. The list labels viginum_pravda and is the known_networks signal. Refitted without it the AUC falls to 0.375; text reuse alone reaches 0.498. Retrieval rarely finds the network's own cross-edition copies (news search does not index *.news-pravda.com).
  • Single-actor rests mostly on fact-check matches. fact_checks alone reaches AUC 0.943; without it 0.841. Techniques alone 0.497, claim support alone 0.806, source profile alone 0.717.
  • Abstention is frequent. Old debunked articles have no coverage in today's feeds, so single-actor abstains on more than half of the positives. The metrics page counts abstentions as coverage, not as wins.
  • Positives skew pro-Kremlin (EUvsDisinfo, VIGINUM); fact-checker debunks of domestic misinformation partly balance this. The set is small: quote the intervals, not only the point values.

Other measurements

  • Technique model outside its training languages (D6): 5 paragraphs in 14 languages; the attack and the fear appeal were found in every language, no neutral paragraph was flagged, and agreement with English was 0.77–0.90 outside the training languages against 0.70–0.80 inside. European languages without labelled data stay “supported, not evaluated” (cap 0.85).
  • Hard-negative regression cases (hand-built records): a fact-check quoting the hoax, emotional disaster reporting, a report quoting insults, agency copy republished by three newsrooms all score 0.02–0.10 single and 0.08 coordinated; the propaganda original scores 0.98 / 0.76.
  • Symmetry probe (10 meme-style text pairs with only the political target swapped, US, UK, DE, IT, FR, ES, immigration, climate, EU): with the phase 2 scorer, 10 of 10 pairs scored on both sides, mean |Δ| 0.011, maximum 0.109 (FR: Renaissance ↔ Rassemblement national, from one technique flagged on one side). Before the refit; see section 6.

6. Persuasion in memes and short posts

A political meme from r/Conservative came back “Not enough evidence” on both dimensions. Memes argue by implication and juxtaposition, the technique model sees nothing in 3- to 8-word text blocks, and single-actor abstained whatever it found because no related coverage existed. The plan (plans/persuasion-analysis.md) splits the fix in two phases.

PhaseContentState
1, score-neutralNew family rhetoric: a multimodal LLM rates persuasion techniques on the text and the image, every quote located verbatim, visual items cite our description of the image; implied claims for fact-check search and matching only; the vision step states what an image implies; image posts on Reddit and X read through vision; web labelsLive. The rater's evidence is shown, not scored
2, the score changerhetoric in the single-actor combiner; single-actor scored on its intrinsic families when coverage is missing; refit with the meme set; metrics and symmetry check in the same PRNot merged (draft #53): the eval run could not finish before the Sun 06:00 cut-off and was stopped. Waits for after submission
  • Meme set. SemEval-2024 Task 4 was rejected: its terms restrict the data to that shared task. We use 99 English memes from the SemEval-2021 Task 6 corpus (“free for general research use”): 64 with at least one annotated persuasion technique, 35 with none. The positive/negative mapping is ours, the negatives are hard ones, and nothing from the dataset is committed. Memes are reported in their own rows, never in the article headline.
  • Observed on phase 1. On two test memes the rater marked a clear “attack on reputation” on both the positive and the negative one: a hint, not a measured false-positive rate, to check in the refit.

10. Limitations

  • Coordinated detection depends on a published list. Without VIGINUM the cross-validated AUC is 0.375. The graph shows the network is findable from copies and translations, but that is not yet in the score.
  • Single-actor depends on fact-checks and fresh coverage. Without a fact-check match the AUC is 0.84, and old or niche items abstain. Persuasion scoring without coverage (phase 2) is built but not fitted.
  • In-sample rates. Precision, recall and FPR are measured on the items the weights were fitted on; only AUCs are cross-validated. A held-out set does not exist yet.
  • Coverage. Single-actor gives a score on 43% of labelled items.
  • The combined number on the result page is not evaluated on its own. It is computed from the two dimension confidences (noisy-OR); the metrics in section 5 are per dimension.
  • Claim-level NLI still produces false contradictions between two moments of one story; a batched LLM check filters the ambiguous ones.
  • Same-language echo chambers. Peers from a closed media ecosystem can corroborate a false claim (EUvsDisinfo false negatives).
  • Label skew. EUvsDisinfo and VIGINUM cover pro-Kremlin operations only.
  • Retrieval gaps. News search does not index the Pravda editions; paywalls and blocked sites fail extract (5 of 7 failures).
  • Throughput. One 2-vCPU server: about a minute per analysis, four at once.
  • Research-only model. The technique model was trained on research-only SemEval data, a licensing question for a public MIT repository.

11. Next steps

  1. Phase 2 of persuasion analysis. Re-run the 284 articles with the rater, run the 99 memes, refit with rhetoric, publish weights.json and metrics.json together with the per-set false-positive check and the symmetry check (#53).
  2. Coordinated without the list. Feeds of the Pravda and topwar.ru editions in retrieve; graph memory as context (“an earlier article of a tied outlet”), fitted with its ablation; report the AUC without known_networks.
  3. Graph verification. Hand-check the 22 strong ties between different operators, then fit the layer weights on verified pairs.
  4. A held-out evaluation set and a larger labelled set beyond pro-Kremlin operations.
  5. Scale. The detector works on any set of sites: a host could run it across hosted domains to surface networks like Portal Kombat.
